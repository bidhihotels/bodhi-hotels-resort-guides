OUR STORY

Hospitality shaped by place and thoughtful stays

Bodhi Hotels & Resorts brings together two individual properties in the Kumbhalgarh region of Rajasthan.

Two properties, clearly distinct

Collective Hotels Bodhi focuses on dome-style glamping and nature-led accommodation, with planet-inspired stays, dining, a Zen Lounge and a private mini-lake. Explore Collective Hotels Bodhi.

Bodhi Boutique By Collective Select is a separate boutique hotel with Deluxe Rooms, Super Deluxe Rooms and Suite Rooms. Its official property information describes its own restaurant, terrace café, pool and event lawn. Explore Bodhi Boutique.
