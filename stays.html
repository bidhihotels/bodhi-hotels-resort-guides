Choose the stay that suits your journey

Room details are kept separate for each Bodhi property.

Dome stays at Collective Hotels Bodhi

The official website lists Neptune, Venus, Uranus, Jupiter and Saturn dome accommodation, alongside a Signature Water Villa and mountain-view room categories. Room sizes and features differ by category. Read the official Collective Hotels Bodhi rooms page.

Rooms at Bodhi Boutique By Collective Select

Bodhi Boutique lists Deluxe Rooms, Super Deluxe Rooms and Suite Rooms. Its official room descriptions distinguish mountain views, private balconies, bathtub and couple jacuzzi features by category. Review the official Bodhi Boutique rooms page.

Before you reserve

Ask the hotel to confirm the exact room category, occupancy, view, jacuzzi or pool access, inclusions, accessibility and cancellation terms for your dates. Do not assume an amenity described for one room is included in another.
