Event information at Bodhi Boutique

The official Bodhi Boutique website describes its lawn as a venue for small gatherings, boutique weddings and corporate events. Venue capacity, timings and availability should be confirmed directly. Official event information.

Enquire about Collective Hotels Bodhi separately

Collective Hotels Bodhi is a separate property with dome accommodation and nature-led experiences. Do not assume that Bodhi Boutique's lawn or event arrangements are available at Collective Hotels Bodhi. Contact the correct property team.

Details to share

  • Preferred dates and approximate guest count
  • Event format and accommodation requirement
  • Meal preferences and service timings
  • Décor, sound, lighting and production needs
  • Parking, access and weather contingency

Ask for a written proposal covering venue access, inclusions, taxes, vendor policies, payment milestones and cancellation terms.
