Luxury Nature Retreats in the Aravalli Hills

What to consider when choosing a nature-led retreat in the Aravalli region, from views and outdoor activities to responsible travel.

The Aravalli landscape gives a Rajasthan holiday a different rhythm. Hills, open skies and changing light invite visitors to slow down between sightseeing plans. A nature retreat should make it easy to enjoy the setting while providing clear information about accommodation, dining and facilities.

Look beyond the view

Ask which room categories actually face the hills, whether the view is seasonal, how much privacy the outdoor space offers and whether the room has the facilities you need. Check walking distances and access if anyone in your group has mobility requirements.

Collective Hotels Bodhi

Collective Hotels Bodhi describes its Kumbhalgarh setting with views towards the Aravalli Mountains and Kumbhalgarh Fort. The property offers dome accommodation and mountain-view room categories. Its official facilities information includes a Zen Lounge, private mini-lake, Taramandal restaurant, Bar - Chuski and indoor games.

Make wellness personal

If you want to use spa, sauna or steam facilities, confirm availability, booking requirements and charges before arrival. A retreat can also be as simple as a slow breakfast, reading outdoors or taking a walk.

Find your preferred style

For dome glamping and nature-led facilities, explore Collective Hotels Bodhi. For a conventional boutique hotel format, consider Bodhi Boutique By Collective Select. They are separate properties with different room types and facilities.
