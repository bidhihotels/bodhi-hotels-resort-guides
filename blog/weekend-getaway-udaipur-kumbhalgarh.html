BODHI TRAVEL JOURNAL · KUMBHALGARH

Weekend Getaway from Udaipur to Kumbhalgarh

Plan a relaxed road getaway from Udaipur to Kumbhalgarh with route checks, a simple itinerary and property comparison tips.

Kumbhalgarh can work well as a short road escape from Udaipur for travellers who want to exchange city routines for a heritage visit and time in the Aravalli region. Plan the journey realistically and avoid overloading a short weekend.

Check your route before departure

Journey time varies with traffic, weather, roadworks and the exact starting point. Use a current navigation service on the day, check fuel and rest stops and confirm the final approach with your hotel. Bodhi Boutique's official about page gives an approximate distance from Udaipur Airport; this is not a guaranteed door-to-door journey time.

A simple weekend plan

Friday evening: travel, check in and keep dinner simple. Saturday: visit Kumbhalgarh Fort after checking current visitor information, then return for a relaxed afternoon. Sunday: enjoy breakfast and begin the return journey with a comfortable buffer.

Choose the right base

Collective Hotels Bodhi is a glamping-focused property with dome accommodation and facilities such as Taramandal, Bar - Chuski, a Zen Lounge and mini-lake. Bodhi Boutique By Collective Select is a separate boutique hotel with Deluxe, Super Deluxe and Suite Rooms, plus its own dining and event spaces.

Confirm before you leave

Verify room availability, check-in timing, meal plan, parking and cancellation terms directly with the property. Use the property overview to compare the two hotels and make reservations through their official channels.

Plan your Bodhi stay

For current room availability, facilities and booking conditions, use the official Bodhi Hotels website.
