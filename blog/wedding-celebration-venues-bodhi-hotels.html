BODHI TRAVEL JOURNAL · KUMBHALGARH

Wedding and Celebration Venues at Bodhi Hotels

Event enquiry considerations for Bodhi Boutique and Collective Hotels Bodhi, with clear distinctions between the properties.

Kumbhalgarh's hill setting can provide a memorable backdrop for a small celebration or multi-day gathering. Before choosing a venue, understand the property's published event information and discuss practical requirements with the hotel team.

Event information at Bodhi Boutique

Bodhi Boutique By Collective Select's official website describes its lawn as a venue for small gatherings, boutique weddings and corporate events. Venue capacity, event timings and availability should be confirmed directly; this guide does not assume a guest limit or package.

Enquire about Collective Hotels Bodhi separately

Collective Hotels Bodhi is a separate property with dome accommodation and nature-led facilities. Do not assume that Bodhi Boutique's lawn or event arrangements are available at Collective Hotels Bodhi. Share your event brief with the correct team.

Details to include

Share preferred dates, approximate guest count, event format, accommodation needs, meal preferences, décor, sound and lighting requirements, parking and weather contingency.

Ask for a written proposal

A clear proposal should explain venue access, inclusions, taxes, vendor policies, payment milestones, cancellation terms, noise restrictions and additional charges. Confirm arrangements in writing before making commitments.
