Resort Facilities and Experiences at Collective Hotels Bodhi

An overview of the dining, wellness, recreation and local experiences published for Collective Hotels Bodhi in Kumbhalgarh.

A resort stay is shaped by more than the room. Dining, recreation and the option to explore the surrounding region can make it easier to settle into a destination. Collective Hotels Bodhi presents facilities and experiences designed around its glamping setting in Kumbhalgarh.

Dining at Taramandal

The official property website describes Taramandal as a 30-cover multi-cuisine restaurant with vegetarian and non-vegetarian options and an outdoor deck for alfresco dining. The site notes fresh ingredients sourced from local farms. Confirm the current menu and service timings before arrival.

Bar - Chuski

Bar - Chuski is described as a coffee bar and sunset lounge, serving coffee, mocktails and snacks with views towards the Aravalli Mountains. Ask about operating hours and current service.

Zen Lounge

The official facilities page lists spa, sauna and steam room facilities within the Zen Lounge. Spa services may require advance booking and may carry separate charges, so confirm the current treatment menu and availability.

Mini-lake and game zone

The property describes a mini-lake with paddle boating. Its indoor game zone lists billiards, table tennis, carrom and chess. Follow staff instructions and ask about age restrictions or operating conditions.

Local experiences

The official website lists village tours, regional cuisine, folk dance performances, trekking, nature walks and visits to Kumbhalgarh Fort. Experiences may depend on season, local partners and weather. These facilities refer to Collective Hotels Bodhi only; Bodhi Boutique has a different facility list.
