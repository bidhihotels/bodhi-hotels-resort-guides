BODHI TRAVEL JOURNAL · KUMBHALGARH

Luxury Glamping Resort in Kumbhalgarh, Rajasthan

A practical guide to dome glamping and nature-led accommodation at Collective Hotels Bodhi.

Collective Hotels Bodhi is a nature-led glamping property in Kumbhalgarh, Rajasthan. Its official accommodation collection includes planet-inspired domes named Neptune, Venus, Uranus, Jupiter and Saturn, along with a Signature Water Villa and mountain-view room categories. Room sizes and features vary, so check the exact category before booking.

What to look for in a glamping stay

Glamping brings an outdoor setting together with hotel-style accommodation. Compare room size, bathroom privacy, air conditioning, bed configuration, views and the facilities included in the quoted rate. Do not assume that every dome includes the same pool, balcony or view.

Facilities at Collective Hotels Bodhi

The property describes Taramandal multi-cuisine restaurant, Bar - Chuski coffee bar and sunset lounge, Zen Lounge, a private mini-lake with paddle boating and an indoor game zone. Experiences listed on its official website include village tours, regional cuisine, folk performances, trekking and nature walks. Confirm operating schedules and charges with the property.

Plan time for Kumbhalgarh

The fort and surrounding Aravalli landscape make a natural part of a visit. Check current attraction timings and road conditions, and leave enough time to enjoy the property rather than rushing between stops.

Is glamping right for you?

A dome stay may appeal to couples, families and travellers looking for distinctive accommodation. Match the room to your group size, mobility needs and preferred facilities.
