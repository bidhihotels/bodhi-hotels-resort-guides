DESTINATION GUIDE

Explore Kumbhalgarh beyond your hotel stay

Heritage, hills and local culture in Rajsamand, Rajasthan.

Kumbhalgarh Fort

Kumbhalgarh Fort is the landmark that brings many travellers to this part of Rajasthan. Check current entry arrangements, visiting hours and guide availability with official site or tourism authorities before setting out. Wear comfortable footwear and carry water, particularly in warmer months.

Nature and local culture

The surrounding countryside and Aravalli landscape can add variety to a visit. Depending on local availability, travellers may look for guided nature walks, village experiences and regional food. Collective Hotels Bodhi lists village tours, folk performances, trekking and nature walks; confirm which experiences are operating.

Getting here

The properties are in the Kumbhalgarh area of Rajsamand district. Road conditions and journey times vary by route, traffic and season. Bodhi Boutique's official about page describes the hotel as around 100 kilometres from Udaipur Airport; confirm the route and transfer time for your travel date.

A simple two-day plan

Day one: Arrive, check in and enjoy an unhurried evening. Day two: Visit Kumbhalgarh Fort, explore the surrounding area and return with time to rest.

Respect heritage-site instructions, avoid littering and follow local guidance on trails. Compare the two Bodhi properties for your stay.
